## Introduction (简介)

简介.

The GLM family welcomes a new generation of open-source models, the **GLM-4-32B-0414** series, featuring 32 billion parameters. Its performance is comparable to OpenAI's GPT series and DeepSeek's V3/R1 series, and it supports very user-friendly local deployment features. GLM-4-32B-Base-0414 was pre-trained on 15T of high-quality data, including a large amount of reasoning-type synthetic data, laying the foundation for subsequent reinforcement learning extensions. In the post-training stage, in addition to human preference alignment for dialogue scenarios, we also enhanced the model's performance in instruction following, engineering code, and function calling using techniques such as rejection sampling and reinforcement learning, strengthening the atomic capabilities required for agent tasks. GLM-4-32B-0414 achieves good results

GLM 家族迎来新一代开源模型 GLM-4-32B-0414 系列, 参数规模 320 亿. 页面说它的表现可以和 OpenAI 的 GPT 系列, DeepSeek 的 V3/R1 系列相比, 而且本地部署很方便. GLM-4-32B-Base-0414 在 15T 高质量数据上预训练, 其中有大量推理类合成数据, 为后面扩展强化学习打基础. 后训练阶段除了面向对话场景的人类偏好对齐, 还用拒绝采样和强化学习等技术加强了指令遵循, 工程代码和函数调用, 补强智能体任务需要的原子能力. GLM-4-32B-0414 取得了不错的效果 (这句话跨到第 3 页).

> **回看:** 这里的 「32 billion parameters」 和第 1 页的 「33B params」 是同一个东西吗?
> 主语不同. 这一句说的是 GLM-4-32B-0414 系列, 不是单独给 Z1 报参数量. Z1 是在 GLM-4-32B-0414 上继续训练得到的, 页面没提训练中改过规模, 所以 32B 可以看成它沿用的名义规模. 第 1 页的 33B 是本仓库文件信息栏上的数. 两个数差 1B, 页面没有说明原因.

> **问:** 15T 是 15T 什么?
> 页面只写 「pre-trained on 15T of high-quality data」, 没写单位. 是 token 数, 字节数还是别的计量, 卡上没交代. 另外这句的主语是 GLM-4-32B-Base-0414, 讲的是底座的预训练, 不是 Z1 自己的训练数据. 数据来源, 语种比例, 合成数据占多少, 都没给.

**GLM-Z1-32B-0414** is a reasoning model with **deep thinking capabilities**. This was developed based on GLM-4-32B-0414 through cold start and extended reinforcement learning, as well as further training of the model on tasks involving mathematics, code, and logic. Compared to the base model, GLM-Z1-32B-0414 significantly improves mathematical abilities and the capability to solve complex tasks. During the training process, we also introduced general reinforcement learning based on pairwise ranking feedback, further enhancing the model's general capabilities.

GLM-Z1-32B-0414 是一个具备深度思考能力的推理模型. 它以 GLM-4-32B-0414 为基础, 经过冷启动和扩展的强化学习, 又在数学, 代码, 逻辑类任务上进一步训练. 和底座模型比, 它的数学能力和解决复杂任务的能力明显提高. 训练中还引入了基于成对排序反馈的通用强化学习, 进一步加强通用能力.

> **确认:** Z1 的训练流程, 卡上具体印了什么?

**GLM-Z1-Rumination-32B-0414** is a deep reasoning model with **rumination capabilities** (benchmarked against OpenAI's Deep Research). The rumination model integrates search tools during its deep thinking process to handle complex tasks and is trained by utilizing multiple rule-based rewards to guide and extend end-to-end reinforcement learning. Z1-Rumination shows significant improvements in researchstyle writing and complex retrieval tasks.

Despite its smaller scale, GLM-Z1-9B-0414 still exhibits excellent capabilities in mathematical reasoning and general tasks. ## Inference Code (推理代码)

推理代码.

Make Sure Using **transforemrs>=4.51.3**.

请确保 transformers 版本不低于 4.51.3. 源文把 transformers 拼成了 「transforemrs」, PDF 文字层也是这个拼法, 是页面原有的笔误, 不是 OCR 出错.

```python
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer

MODEL_PATH = "THUDM/GLM-4-Z1-32B-0414"

tokenizer = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(MODEL_PATH)
model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(MODEL_PATH, device_map="a

message = [{"role": "user", "content": "Let a, b be positive real number")

inputs = tokenizer.apply_chat_template(
    message,
    return_tensors="pt",
    add_generation_prompt=True,
    return_dict=True,
).to(model.device)

generate_kwargs = {
    "input_ids": inputs["input_ids"],
    "attention_mask": inputs["attention_mask"],
    "max_new_tokens": 4096,
    "do_sample": False,
}
out = model.generate(**generate_kwargs)
print(tokenizer.decode(out[0][inputs["input_ids"].shape[1]:], skip_spec)
```

示例代码: 从 transformers 导入 AutoModelForCausalLM 和 AutoTokenizer, 用 MODEL_PATH 「THUDM/GLM-4-Z1-32B-0414」 加载分词器和模型, 构造一条用户消息, 用 apply_chat_template 生成输入, 再以 max_new_tokens 4096, do_sample False 调用 generate, 最后只解码新生成的部分. 有三行在右端被截断: device_map=「a, 」Let a, b be positive real number", skip_spec. 渲染页上这个代码框底部有横向滚动条, 打印时只印出了可见的宽度.

> **对一下:** 第 4 到 5 页推荐 temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95, top_k 40, max_new_tokens 30000, 示例代码为什么是 do_sample False 和 4096?
> 页面没解释. do_sample 为 False 就是不采样, 推荐的 temperature, top_p, top_k 在这段代码里用不上; max_new_tokens 4096 还不到推荐值 30000 的七分之一. 照这段代码直接跑, 用的并不是第一节推荐的设置. 两处都照录, 实际使用以哪处为准, 卡上没说.

> **再看:** md 里的代码和 PDF 有没有出入?
> 有两处. 一是 md 的 message 那一行写成 `"Let a, b be positive real number")`, 末尾多出一个右括号, 引号也没闭合; PDF 文字层这一行截在 「numbe」, 没有右括号, 右括号是 MinerU 自己补的. 二是 device_map 那一行 md 截在 「a」, PDF 截在 「au」. 两处都只是截断位置不同, 代码的意思没变. 这段代码的组织名是 THUDM, 和标题栏的 zai-org 不一致, 第 2 页拆开那一条已经列过.
