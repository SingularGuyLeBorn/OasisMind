## MiniCPM-o 2.6 (模型简介)

**MiniCPM-o 2.6** is the latest and most capable model in the MiniCPM-o series. The model is built in an end-to-end fashion based on SigLip-400M, Whisper-medium-300M, ChatTTS-200M, and Qwen2.5-7B with a total of 8B parameters. It exhibits a significant performance improvement over MiniCPM-V 2.6, and introduces new features for real-time speech conversation and multimodal live streaming. Notable features of MiniCPM-o 2.6 include:

**MiniCPM-o 2.6** 是 MiniCPM-o 系列里最新, 能力最强的模型. 它以端到端方式搭建, 基于 SigLip-400M, Whisper-medium-300M, ChatTTS-200M 和 Qwen2.5-7B, 总参数 8B. 相比 MiniCPM-V 2.6 性能明显提升, 并新增实时语音对话和多模态实时流两项功能. 主要特点如下:

> **核对:** 这里说总参数 8B, 第 1 页侧栏却显示 9B params, 哪个对?
> 四个组件名字里的数加起来是 0.4B + 0.3B + 0.2B + 7B = 7.9B, 和正文的 8B 对得上. 侧栏的 9B 是 Hugging Face 按权重文件自动统计后取整的数, 名字里的 7B, 400M 都是标称值, 组件之间的连接层也要占参数, 源文没有逐模块的参数表, 这 27 页里分不出多出来的部分是什么. 第 7 页评测表的 Size 列一律写 8B, 按正文口径读.

**Leading Visual Capability.** MiniCPM-o 2.6 achieves an average score of 70.2 on OpenCompass, a comprehensive evaluation over 8 popular benchmarks.**With only 8B parameters, it surpasses widely used proprietary models like GPT-4o-202405, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Claude 3.5 Sonnet** for single image understanding. It also **outperforms GPT-4V and Claude 3.5 Sonnet** in mutli-image and video understanding, and shows promising in-context learning capability.

**领先的视觉能力.** MiniCPM-o 2.6 在 OpenCompass 上平均分 70.2, OpenCompass 是覆盖 8 个常用基准的综合评测.**只用 8B 参数, 它在单图理解上就超过了 GPT-4o-202405, Gemini 1.5 Pro 和 Claude 3.5 Sonnet 这些常用闭源模型.** 在多图和视频理解上它也 **超过 GPT-4V 和 Claude 3.5 Sonnet**, 并表现出不错的上下文学习能力.

**State-of-the-art Speech Capability.** MiniCPM-o 2.6 supports **bilingual real-time speech conversation with configurable voices** in English and Chinese. It **outperforms GPT-4o-realtime on audio understanding tasks** such as ASR and STT translation, and shows **state-of-the-art performance on speech conversation in both semantic and acoustic evaluations in the open-source community**. It also allows for fun features such as emotion/speed/style control, end-to-end voice cloning, role play, etc.

**最先进的语音能力.** MiniCPM-o 2.6 支持 **中英双语, 音色可配置的实时语音对话**. 在 ASR (语音识别) 和语音到文本翻译这类音频理解任务上, 它 **超过 GPT-4o-realtime**; 在语音对话的语义评测和声学评测上, 它 **在开源社区里达到最先进水平**. 它还支持一些好玩的功能, 比如控制情绪, 语速和风格, 端到端声音克隆, 角色扮演等.

**Strong Multimodal Live Streaming Capability.** As a new feature, MiniCPM-o 2.6 can **accept continous video and audio streams independent of user queries, and support real-time speech interaction**.

**强大的多模态实时流能力.** 作为新功能, MiniCPM-o 2.6 可以 **接收与用户提问无关, 持续不断的视频流和音频流, 并支持实时语音交互**. 这句话在 PDF 里跨到了第 4 页, 这里把后半句接回来.

It **outperforms GPT-4o-202408 and Claude 3.5 Sonnet and shows state-of-art performance in open-source community on StreamingBench**, a comprehensive benchmark for real-time video understanding, omni-source (video & audio) understanding, and multimodal contextual understanding.

在 StreamingBench 上, 它 **超过 GPT-4o-202408 和 Claude 3.5 Sonnet, 并在开源社区里达到最先进水平**. StreamingBench 是一个综合基准, 覆盖实时视频理解, 全源 (视频加音频) 理解和多模态上下文理解三方面.

**Strong OCR Capability and Others.** Advancing popular visual capabilites from MiniCPM-V series, MiniCPM-o 2.6 can process images with any aspect ratio and up to 1.8 million pixels (e.g., 1344x1344). It achieves **state-of-the-art performance on OCRBench for models under 25B, surpassing proprietary models such as GPT-4o-202405**. Based on the the latest [RLAIF-V](https://github.com/RLHF-V/RLAIF-V/) and [VisCPM](https://github.com/OpenBMB/VisCPM) techniques, it features **trustworthy behaviors**, outperforming GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on MMHal-Bench, and supports **multilingual capabilities** on more than 30 languages.

**强大的 OCR 能力及其他.** MiniCPM-o 2.6 延续了 MiniCPM-V 系列受欢迎的视觉能力, 能处理任意长宽比, 最高 180 万像素 (例如 1344x1344) 的图像. 它在 **25B 以下模型里取得 OCRBench 最先进成绩, 超过 GPT-4o-202405 等闭源模型**. 基于最新的 RLAIF-V 和 VisCPM 技术, 它具有 **可信的行为**, 在 MMHal-Bench 上超过 GPT-4o 和 Claude 3.5 Sonnet, 并支持 30 多种语言的 **多语言能力**.

**Superior Efficiency.** In addition to its friendly size, MiniCPM-o 2.6 also shows **state-of-the-art token density** (i.e., number of pixels encoded into each visual token).**It produces only 640 tokens when processing a 1.8M pixel image, which is 75% fewer than most models**. This directly improves the inference speed, first-token latency, memory usage, and power consumption. As a result, MiniCPM-o 2.6 can efficiently support **multimodal live streaming** on end-side devices such as iPad.

**出色的效率.** 除了体量友好, MiniCPM-o 2.6 还有 **最先进的 token 密度** (即每个视觉 token 编码的像素数).**处理一张 180 万像素的图只产生 640 个 token, 比大多数模型少 75%.** 这直接改善了推理速度, 首 token 延迟, 内存占用和功耗. 因此 MiniCPM-o 2.6 能在 iPad 这类端侧设备上高效支持 **多模态实时流**.

> **想:** 1.8M 像素只出 640 个 token, 和第 7 页表里的 token 密度 2822 是不是同一件事?
> 是. 1344 × 1344 = 1,806,336 像素, 除以 640 得 2822.4, 正好是表里 MiniCPM-V2.6 和 MiniCPM-o2.6 的 Token Density. 反过来用密度估别的模型处理同一张图要多少 token: InternVL2.5-8B (706) 约 2558 个, Qwen2-VL-7B (784) 约 2304 个, GPT-4o (1088) 约 1660 个. 640 比 2558 少 75%, 比 2304 少 72%, 比 1660 少 61%. 「少 75%」 大致是拿 InternVL2.5 这一档开源模型比的, 和闭源模型比差距小一些, 而闭源模型的密度按第 7 页注释是按计费规则估的上界.

**Easy Usage.** MiniCPM-o 2.6 can be easily used in various ways: (1) [llama.cpp](https://github.com/OpenBMB/llama.cpp/blob/minicpm-omni/examples/llava/README-minicpmo2.6.md) support for efficient CPU inference on local devices, (2) [int4](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-o-2_6-int4) and [GGUF](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-o-2_6-gguf) format quantized models in 16 sizes, (3) vLLM support for high-throughput and memory-efficient inference, (4) fine-tuning on new domains and tasks with [LLaMA-Factory](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-o-2_6/blob/main/docs/llamafactory_train.md), (5) quick local WebUI demo setup with <u>Gradio</u>, and (6) online web demo on [server](https://minicpm-omni-webdemo-us.modelbest.cn/).

**易于使用.** MiniCPM-o 2.6 有多种用法: (1) 用 llama.cpp 在本地设备上做高效的 CPU 推理; (2) int4 和 GGUF 格式的量化模型, 共 16 种尺寸; (3) 用 vLLM 做高吞吐, 省内存的推理; (4) 用 LLaMA-Factory 在新领域, 新任务上微调; (5) 用 Gradio 快速在本地搭 WebUI 演示; (6) 服务器上的在线网页演示.

## Model Architecture. (模型架构)

**End-to-end Omni-modal Architecture.** Different modality encoder/decoders are connected and trained in an **end-to-end** fashion to fully exploit rich multimodal knowledge.

**端到端全模态架构.** 不同模态的编码器和解码器连接在一起, 以 **端到端** 方式训练, 以充分利用丰富的多模态知识.

<!-- page 5 of 27 -->

**Omni-modal Live Streaming Mechanism.** (1) We change the offline modality encoder/decoders into online ones for **streaminig inputs/outputs.** (2) We devise a **time-division multiplexing (TDM) mechanism** for omni-modality streaminig processing in the LLM backbone. It divides parallel omni-modality streams into sequential info within small periodic time slices.

**全模态实时流机制.** (1) 把离线的各模态编码器和解码器改成在线的, 支持 **流式输入和输出**. (2) 在 LLM 主干里设计了一套 **时分复用 (TDM) 机制** 来处理全模态流: 把并行的多路模态流, 切成小的周期性时间片, 变成按顺序排列的信息.

> **问:** 时分复用把各路流切成 「小的周期性时间片」, 一片有多长?
> 架构段落没有给数. 第 15 页 Omni mode 示例代码里有一行注释 「# 1 frame + 1s audio chunk」: 函数按视频时长向上取整, 每一秒取 1 帧画面和 1 秒 16kHz 音频, 前面加一个 「<unit>」 标记拼成一个单元. 这是用法示例里的切法, 说明对外接口按秒喂数据, 模型内部是否也以 1 秒为一片, 源文没有明说.

**Configurable Speech Modeling Design.** We devise a multimodal system prompt, including traditional text system prompt, and **a new audio system prompt to determine the assistant voice**. This enables flexible voice configurations in inference time, and also facilitates end-to-end voice cloning and description-based voice creation.

**可配置的语音建模设计.** 我们设计了多模态系统提示, 其中既有传统的文本系统提示, 也有 **一段新的音频系统提示, 用来决定助手的音色**. 这样推理阶段就能灵活配置音色, 也方便做端到端声音克隆和按文字描述创建声音.

![MiniCPM-o 2.6 全模态流式架构示意图](images/p05-evaluation.png)

这张图 MinerU 按紧跟的标题命名成 evaluation, 内容其实是架构示意图. 底部是时间轴 t1 到 t13; 往上三行依次是用户提问音频 Query^A, 音频流 Stream^A, 视觉流 Stream^V. 左侧的 OTDM (Omni-modality Time Division Multiplexer, 全模态时分复用器) 把每个时刻的一帧画面和一段音频交替排成一串, 送进 Omni-Modality Streaming Backbone (全模态流式主干). 用户在 t3 到 t4 问 「What just ran past?」 (刚才跑过去的是什么?), 主干在 t5 输出 H0 和 T1 T2 T3; 图例说明 H0 是 「LLM Hidden as Speech Embedding」 (把 LLM 隐状态当作语音嵌入), T 是文本 token. 最上层的 Streaming Speech Decoder (流式语音解码器) 据此说出 「A cat just ran out, and a dog is going by as you speak.」 (一只猫刚跑出去, 你说话的时候正好有只狗经过). t6 到 t8 两路输入标成 ∅ (Omitted Inputs, 省略的输入). t11 用户说 「Read a poem happily.」 (开心地读一首诗), t12 主干再次输出 H0 和 T1 T2, 解码器进入 <speaking>, t13 又标 ∅. 图只画了模块和数据流向, 没有给层数, 维度或时间片长度.

**MiniCPM-o 2.6**

To find the intersection points of two functions, we need to solve for x in the equation where both functions are equal. The given functions are:

模型回答: 要找两个函数的交点, 就要解两函数相等时的 x. 给出的两个函数是:

$$
1.\ f(x) = x^{3} + 3x^{2} - 2x + 1
$$

$$
2.\ g(x) = x^{2} + x + 1
$$

Firstly, set them equal:

先令两者相等:

$$
x^{3} + 3x^{2} - 2x + 1 = x^{2} + x + 1
$$

Simplify by combining like terms:

合并同类项化简:

$$
x^{3} + 3x^{2} - 2x + 1 - x^{2} - x - 1 = 0
$$

$$
x^{3} + 2x^{2} - 3x = 0
$$

Factor out an x:

提出公因子 x:

$$
x(x^{2} + 2x - 3) = 0
$$

This gives us three potential solutions:

由此得到三个可能的解:

$$
\begin{array}{c} x = 0 \\ x^{2} + 2x - 3 = 0 \end{array}
$$

Solve the quadratic equation using the quadratic formula:

用求根公式解这个二次方程:

$$
a = 1,\ b = 2,\ c = -3
$$

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^{2} - 4ac}}{2a} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 12}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{16}}{2} = \frac{-2 \pm 4}{2}
$$

This results in:

结果是:

$$
x = 1
$$

$$
x = -3
$$

For each value of x, plug it back into either function to get the corresponding y-values.

把每个 x 代回任一函数, 求出对应的 y 值.

For x = 0:

x = 0 时:

$$
\begin{array}{c} f(0) = (0)^{3} + 3(0)^{2} - 2(0) + 1 = 1 \\ g(0) = (0)^{2} + (0) + 1 = 1 \end{array}
$$

For x = 1:

x = 1 时:

$$
\begin{array}{c} f(1) = (1)^{3} + 3(1)^{2} - 2(1) + 1 = 1 + 3 - 2 + 1 = 3 \\ g(1) = (1)^{2} + (1) + 1 = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3 \end{array}
$$

For x = -3:

x = -3 时:

$$
\begin{array}{c} f(-3) = (-3)^{3} + 3(-3)^{2} - 2(-3) + 1 = -27 + 27 + 6 + 1 = 7 \\ g(-3) = (-3)^{2} + (-3) + 1 = 9 - 3 + 1 = 7 \end{array}
$$

Thus, the coordinates of the intersection points are:

1. (0, 1)
2. (1, 3)
3. (-3, 7)

所以交点坐标是 (0, 1), (1, 3), (-3, 7). 上面的公式按 PDF 渲染出的原图整理: MinerU 把两个函数的编号顺序排反, 把多处减号识别成中点, 还丢了求根公式的分母 2a 和中间三步代入, 这里都按原图补回.

> **拆开:** 这道交点题的答案对不对?
