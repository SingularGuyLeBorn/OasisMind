# Baichuan-M3: Modeling Clinical Inquiry for Reliable Medical Decision-Making 为临床问诊建模, 走向可靠的医学决策

[Baichuan-M3 Team](https://www.baichuan-ai.com/)

## Abstract

We introduce Baichuan-M3, a medical-enhanced large language model engineered to shift the paradigm from passive question-answering to active, clinical-grade decision support. Addressing the limitations of existing systems in open-ended consultations, Baichuan-M3 utilizes a specialized training pipeline to model the systematic workflow of a physician. Key capabilities include: (i) proactive information acquisition to resolve ambiguity; (ii) long-horizon reasoning that unifies scattered evidence into coherent diagnoses; and (iii) adaptive hallucination suppression to ensure factual reliability. Empirical evaluations demonstrate that Baichuan-M3 achieves state-of-the-art results on HealthBench, the newly introduced HealthBench-Hallu and ScanBench, significantly outperforming GPT-5.2 in clinical inquiry, advisory and safety. The models are publicly available at [https://huggingface.co/collections/baichuan-inc/baichuan-m3](https://huggingface.co/collections/baichuan-inc/baichuan-m3).

我们推出 Baichuan-M3, 一个经过医学增强的大语言模型, 目标是把范式从被动问答推向主动的, 临床级的决策支持. 现有系统在开放式问诊中存在局限, 针对这一点, Baichuan-M3 用一条专门的训练流水线来模拟医生的系统化工作流程. 其关键能力包括: (i) 主动获取信息以消除歧义; (ii) 长程推理, 把零散证据整合成连贯的诊断; (iii) 自适应的幻觉抑制, 保证事实可靠. 实证评测表明, Baichuan-M3 在 HealthBench, 新提出的 HealthBench-Hallu 与 ScanBench 上取得 state-of-the-art 结果, 在临床问诊, 咨询建议与安全性上显著超过 GPT-5.2. 模型已公开发布于 [https://huggingface.co/collections/baichuan-inc/baichuan-m3](https://huggingface.co/collections/baichuan-inc/baichuan-m3).

## 1 Introduction

Large language models (LLMs) are advancing rapidly [1–4], driving broader adoption in healthcare [5–9]. As a result, expectations are shifting from one-off question answering to end-to-end clinical decision support [10, 11]. This trend is reflected in leading systems such as OpenAI’s GPT-5.2 [12], ChatGPT Health [13], and Claude in Healthcare [14]. Yet key limitations persist: despite better scores on static, well-specified benchmarks, models often fail to stay evidence-grounded and uncertainty-aware in open-ended clinical interactions, where missing information and longhorizon decisions make hallucinations harder to control [15]. Our goal is therefore to move beyond reliable QA toward decision-support partners that can operate safely in practice.

大语言模型 (LLM) 进展迅速 [1–4], 推动它在医疗领域更广泛地落地 [5–9]. 人们的期待随之从一次性问答转向端到端的临床决策支持 [10, 11]. OpenAI 的 GPT-5.2 [12], ChatGPT Health [13] 与 Claude in Healthcare [14] 等领先系统都体现了这一趋势. 但关键局限依然存在: 模型在静态, 定义明确的基准上分数更高, 到了开放式临床交互中却常常守不住证据, 也不能恰当表达不确定性; 在这类交互里, 信息缺失与长程决策让幻觉更难控制 [15]. 因此, 我们的目标是越过 「可靠问答」, 走向能在实践中安全运行的决策支持伙伴.

To bridge the gap between passive retrieval and active clinical support, recent studies have been

Google 的 AMIE [18, 19] 等系统在这些评分细则下的模拟接诊中展现了很强的沟通质量.

However, a critical gap remains in unifying these two dimensions. Existing approaches often treat conversational interaction and clinical reasoning as orthogonal objectives, rather than components of a single coherent system. Knowledge-centric models exhibit “inquiry inertia,” lacking the agency to elicit missing evidence, while interaction-focused models can sacrifice diagnostic depth, prioritizing fluency over principled differential reasoning.

然而, 如何把这两个维度统一起来, 仍是一个关键缺口. 现有方法往往把对话交互与临床推理当作正交的目标, 而不是同一个连贯系统的组成部分. 以知识为中心的模型表现出 「问诊惰性」, 缺乏主动引出缺失证据的能动性; 以交互为中心的模型则可能牺牲诊断深度, 把流畅度放在有原则的鉴别推理之前.

Integrating interaction and reasoning is challenging due to three technical bottlenecks. First, heterogeneous training environments across diverse clinical tasks hinder stable multi-task fusion. Second, long-horizon interactions amplify the credit-assignment problem in reinforcement learn ing (RL) [20–22]: when supervision is dominated by terminal outcomes, models struggle to identify which conversational turns were causally responsible for diagnostic success. Third, efforts to in crease reasoning depth often encounter reward saturation, where learning signals diminish near performance plateaus—sometimes accompanied by increased hallucination as models attempt to satisfy complex logical constraints [23, 24].

把交互与推理整合起来并不容易, 卡在三个技术瓶颈上. 第一, 不同临床任务的训练环境彼此异构, 妨碍稳定的多任务融合. 第二, 长程交互放大了强化学习 (RL) 中的信用分配问题 [20–22]: 当监督主要来自最终结果时, 模型很难分辨是哪几轮对话对诊断成功起了因果作用. 第三, 提升推理深度的努力常会遇到奖励饱和, 学习信号在性能平台附近逐渐减弱, 有时还伴随幻觉增加, 因为模型在努力满足复杂的逻辑约束 [23, 24].

To address these challenges, we introduce Baichuan-M3, a next-generation medical LLM designed to unify clinical inquiry with reliable decision-making. Baichuan-M3 emphasizes three core competencies aligned with real clinical workflows: (i) proactive information acquisition, (ii) construction of coherent reasoning trajectories, and (iii) adaptive hallucination suppression.

为应对这些挑战, 我们推出新一代医学大模型 Baichuan-M3, 旨在把临床问诊与可靠决策统一起来. Baichuan-M3 强调与真实临床流程对齐的三项核心能力: (i) 主动获取信息, (ii) 构建连贯的推理轨迹, (iii) 自适应的幻觉抑制.

Methodologically, we propose a three-stage training framework consisting of Task-Specific Rein forcement Learning (TaskRL), Offline Policy Distillation, and Multi-Teacher Online Policy Distillation (MOPD). This hierarchical design decouples the optimization of individual competencies before integrating them into a single policy. To improve long-horizon consultation performance, we introduce Segmented Pipeline Reinforcement Learning, which decomposes complex tasks into stages with separate reward signals. We further strengthen diagnostic reasoning via Dynamic Rubric Evolution and targeted RL objectives for hallucination suppression.

方法上, 我们提出一个三阶段训练框架, 依次为任务专项强化学习 (TaskRL), 离线策略蒸馏, 以及多教师在线策略蒸馏 (MOPD). 这种分层设计先把各项能力的优化解耦, 再把它们整合进单一策略. 为提升长程问诊表现, 我们引入分段流水线强化学习 (Segmented Pipeline RL), 把复杂任务拆成若干阶段, 每个阶段有独立的奖励信号. 我们还通过动态 rubric 演化 (Dynamic Rubric Evolution) 与针对幻觉抑制的 RL 目标, 进一步加强诊断推理.

## 2 Training Infrastructure 训练基础设施

In this section, we describe the training infrastructure of Baichuan-M3, including a stable patient simulation environment, a verification system that integrates rubric-based and fact-aware evaluation, and a progressive multi-stage training pipeline. Together, these components provide reliable interaction signals and scalable optimization support for long-horizon medical training.

本节介绍 Baichuan-M3 的训练基础设施, 包括稳定的患者模拟环境, 融合 rubric 评估与事实感知评估的验证系统, 以及渐进式的多阶段训练流水线. 这些组件共同为长程医学训练提供可靠的交互信号与可扩展的优化支持.

produce. To balance the generalization benefits of stochasticity with the stability required for long-horizon training, we adopt a passive-personality patient simulator and implement two complementary script-driven modes, referred to as Passive Interaction Mode and Interruption-Injected Mode.

在前作 [6] 中, 我们提出了一个用于医患交互的患者模拟器. 部署过程中我们发现, 模拟主动型患者时, 模拟器会变得不稳定. 具体来说, 这类行为会打乱问诊流程, 让模拟难以复现. 为在随机性带来的泛化收益与长程训练所需的稳定性之间取得平衡, 我们采用被动人格的患者模拟器, 并实现两种互补的脚本驱动模式, 分别称为被动交互模式 (Passive Interaction Mode) 与插话注入模式 (Interruption-Injected Mode).

**Passive Interaction Mode (75% sampling probability):** This mode provides only the patient profile, inquiry rubrics, and behavioral constraints rubrics, without any predefined dialogue history. It simulates the opening phase of an initial consultation. Starting from an empty interaction state, this mode evaluates the physician agent’s ability to proactively elicit relevant information and form an initial diagnostic hypothesis under high uncertainty.

**被动交互模式 (采样概率 75%):** 该模式只提供患者档案, 问诊 rubric 与行为约束 rubric, 不带任何预设的对话历史, 模拟初诊的开场阶段. 从空的交互状态出发, 该模式考察医生 agent 在高度不确定下主动引出相关信息, 形成初步诊断假设的能力.

**Interruption-Injected Mode (25% sampling probability):** This mode augments the basic patient information with a predefined dialogue snippet to simulate a mid-consultation state. The snippet ends with a patient-initiated question (often anxiety-driven about severity or treatment), modeling a common interruption during inquiry. However, exposing this snippet to the patient simulator may cause it to mimic the snippet’s speaking style and deviate from our passive-response protocol, introducing instability. We therefore use an asymmetric visibility mechanism: the snippet is visible only to the physician agent and hidden from the patient simulator. The physician answers the question and continues the consultation, while the simulator passively receives and responds to subsequent queries.

**插话注入模式 (采样概率 25%):** 该模式在基本患者信息之外, 附加一段预设的对话片段, 模拟问诊进行到一半的状态. 片段以患者主动提出的问题结尾 (多是出于焦虑, 询问病情轻重或治疗方案), 模拟问诊中常见的插话. 然而, 把这段片段暴露给患者模拟器, 可能导致它模仿片段的说话风格, 偏离我们的被动应答协议, 带来不稳定. 因此我们采用非对称可见机制: 片段只对医生 agent 可见, 对患者模拟器隐藏. 医生回答这个问题并继续问诊, 模拟器则被动接收并回应后续提问.

To reduce distribution mismatch between the simulated environment and real-world online consultations, we further introduce fine-grained probabilistic configurations in Interruption-Injected Mode. Specifically, end-of-turn questioning accounts for 50% of cases, where the patient question appears only at the end of the snippet, testing the model’s ability to handle abrupt interruptions. The remaining 50% corresponds to mid-turn questioning, which injects questions within an ongoing turn to simulate multi-turn. This mixed configuration ensures robust diagnostic performance under interaction noise, including frequent interruptions, challenges, and anxiety-driven follow-up questions.

为缩小模拟环境与真实线上问诊之间的分布差异, 我们在插话注入模式中进一步引入细粒度的概率配置. 具体而言, 轮末提问占 50%, 患者问题只出现在片段结尾, 用来考察模型应对突然插话的能力; 其余 50% 为轮中提问, 在进行中的某一轮里插入问题, 模拟多轮交织. 这种混合配置确保模型在交互噪声下仍保持稳健的诊断表现, 噪声包括频繁插话, 质疑以及出于焦虑的追问.

To guarantee the reliability and clinical safety of the generated responses, we construct a comprehensive Verify System that serves as the primary source of reward signals for Reinforcement Learning. Unlike general-domain chat models where fluency and helpfulness are often sufficient,

A monolithic reward model often struggles to disentangle these orthogonal dimensions—potentially encouraging fluent hallucinations over rigid accuracy. To address this, our system decouples the evaluation process into two parallel streams: a **Rubric Verifier** that assesses the structural quality and adherence to clinical guidelines through fine-grained criteria, and a **Fact Verifier** that rigorously checks the biological and medical validity of atomic claims against external authoritative sources. #### 2.2.1 Rubric Verifier Rubric 验证器

The M3 verification stack follows the rubric-based verifier paradigm [25, 26] introduced in M2 [6]. Instead of treating medical response quality as a single monolithic preference signal, we decompose each interaction into a set of independently decidable rubric clauses. An LLM-based judge [27] evaluates each clause item by item, and the resulting decisions are aggregated into a scalar reward for policy optimization.

M3 的验证栈沿用 M2 [6] 引入的基于 rubric 的验证器范式 [25, 26]. 我们不把医学回答质量当作单一的整体偏好信号, 而是把每次交互拆成一组可独立判定的 rubric 条款. 一个基于 LLM 的裁判 [27] 逐条评估这些条款, 再把各条判定聚合成一个标量奖励, 用于策略优化.

Given a sample x and a set of rubrics $\begin{array} { r } { \mathcal { R }   =   \{ r _ { i } \} _ { i = 1 } ^ { N } , } \end{array}$ each rubric $r _ { i }$ is assigned a signed weight $w_i \in [-10, 10]$ , where $w _ { i }   >   0$ denotes rewards and $w _ { i }   <   0$ denotes penalties. An LLM judge outputs a binary decision $a _ { i } \in \{ 0 , 1 \}$ indicating whether $r _ { i }$ is satisfied. The task reward is obtained by min-max normalization:

给定样本 x 与一组 rubric $\mathcal{R} = \{r_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$, 每条 rubric $r_i$ 带一个有符号权重 $w_i \in [-10, 10]$, $w_i > 0$ 表示奖励, $w_i < 0$ 表示惩罚. LLM 裁判输出二元判定 $a_i \in \{0, 1\}$, 表示 $r_i$ 是否被满足. 任务奖励通过 min-max 归一化得到:

$$
R _ {\mathrm{task}} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {N} w _ {i} a _ {i} - \sum_ {i : w _ {i} <   0} w _ {i}}{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {N} | w _ {i} |}\tag{1}
$$

This normalization decouples the reward scale from both the number of rubrics and the magnitude of their weights into [0, 1]. As a result, reward distributions remain comparable across different samples and rubric configurations, while integer weights can be used to directly encode relative clause importance in an interpretable manner.

这种归一化把奖励尺度同时从条款数量与权重大小中解耦出来, 统一落到 [0, 1]. 于是, 不同样本, 不同 rubric 配置下的奖励分布保持可比, 而整数权重又可以直接, 可解释地编码各条款的相对重要性.

> **拆开:** 式 (1) 里负权重条款的 $a_i=1$ 表示什么? 把分子拆成正负两组以后, 「与条款数量解耦」 到底解耦了哪一层?
> 按 §2.2.1 的定义, $a_i=1$ 只表示条款 $r_i$ 被判定为 「满足」; 对 $w_i<0$ 的惩罚条款, 「满足」 就是触发了被惩罚的行为. 把分子改写成 $\sum_{w_i>0} w_i a_i + \sum_{w_i<0} |w_i|(1-a_i)$ 就能看清: 正条款全部满足, 负条款一个都没触发时, 分子等于 $\sum|w_i|$, 奖励为 1; 反过来为 0. 所以式 (1) 保证的是取值范围落在 [0, 1], 翻转任意一条条款带来的变化仍是 $|w_i|/\sum_j|w_j|$. 条款越少, 单条条款的分量越重, 这一点归一化并没有抹掉. 正文说的 「decouple」 指不同样本的奖励尺度可比, 不指每条条款的敏感度一致.

To increase the efficiency in the RL pipeline, reward evaluation is scheduled asynchronously with the rollout process: once the policy emits a response, we immediately launch the corresponding rubric-judging jobs, overlapping judge compute with other unconflicted RL actions (e.g., computing $\pi _ { \mathrm { r e f } } / \pi _ { \mathrm { o l d } }$ log-probabilities) to accelerate the training. In addition, we adopt a prefix-affinity prompt design for the judge: system constraints, output schema, and dialog context share the same template prefix, while the suffixes are substituted with the rubric clause. This processing performs micro-batching to maximize KV-cache [28] reuse, substantially reducing the time cost.

#### 2.2.2 Fact Verifier 事实验证器

Baichuan-M2 [6] mainly relies on rubric-based rewards to shape medical reasoning. As performance saturates on common criteria, the model tends to chase marginal gains by adding rarer clinical details, which increases hallucination risk; we therefore optimize for both completeness and reliability. To make the objective of a low hallucination rate quantitative and optimizable, we build upon existing long-form factuality evaluation methodologies [29, 30] to construct a **Fact-Aware Verification Pipeline**. The pipeline adopts a two-stage architecture: Firstly, we decompose long-form responses into fine-grained, independently verifiable atomic claims. Secondly, we employ a search-augmented verification agent to validate each claim against authoritative sources. This module runs in parallel with the Rubric Verifier as a service, minimizing latency.

Baichuan-M2 [6] 主要依靠基于 rubric 的奖励塑造医学推理. 当常见条款上的表现趋于饱和, 模型倾向于补充更罕见的临床细节来追逐边际收益, 这会抬高幻觉风险; 因此我们同时优化完整性与可靠性. 为了让 「低幻觉率」 这个目标可量化, 可优化, 我们在已有的长文本事实性评估方法 [29, 30] 基础上, 构建了 **事实感知验证流水线 (Fact-Aware Verification Pipeline)**. 该流水线分两步: 第一步, 把长篇回答拆成细粒度的, 可独立验证的原子论断 (atomic claim); 第二步, 用检索增强的验证 agent 依据权威来源逐条核验. 该模块作为服务与 Rubric 验证器并行运行, 尽量降低延迟.

**原子论断抽取模型.** 流水线的基础, 是把非结构化的模型输出转成离散的, 可验证的单元, 即原子论断. 这些论断必须自成一体, 脱离原始上下文也能被验证. 为满足这一要求, 我们采用以下规则:

1. **Atomicity and Coreference Resolution.** We decompose complex compound sentences into single-fact units and resolve pronouns to guarantee semantic independence.

   **原子化与指代消解.** 把复杂的复合句拆成单一事实单元, 并把代词还原为所指对象, 保证语义独立.

2. **Noise and Distractor Filtering.** We discard units that lack sufficient context to form a factual statement. Crucially, in multiple-choice scenarios, we explicitly exclude the recitation of incorrect options (distractors) to prevent them from being misclassified as hallucinations.

   **噪声与干扰项过滤.** 丢弃上下文不足以构成事实陈述的单元. 关键的一点是, 在选择题场景中, 我们明确排除对错误选项 (干扰项) 的复述, 防止它们被误判为幻觉.

3. **Deduplication with Order Preservation.** Semantically redundant assertions are eliminated while maintaining the original logical sequence of the reasoning chain.

   **保序去重.** 去掉语义上重复的断言, 同时保持推理链原有的逻辑顺序.

In the evaluation phase, we initially use GPT-5 [31] as a high-quality extractor; however, its inference latency is prohibitive for online RL. We therefore distill an efficient 8B extraction model from GPT-5, trained on datasets spanning multiple medical subtasks, and use it as the final extractor, with detailed fidelity evaluation provided in Appendix A.2.1.

在评估阶段, 我们起初用 GPT-5 [31] 作为高质量抽取器; 但它的推理延迟对在线 RL 来说难以承受. 因此我们从 GPT-5 蒸馏出一个高效的 8B 抽取模型, 训练数据覆盖多个医学子任务, 并把它用作最终的抽取器, 详细的保真度评估见附录 A.2.1.

## 3 Task-specific Training Methods 任务专项训练方法

Clinical practice encompasses a diverse spectrum of cognitive activities, ranging from the active elicitation of symptom history to the rigorous provision of evidence-based advice. A monolithic training approach often fails to balance the distinct requirements of these scenarios—specifically, the deductive logic required for diagnosis versus the strict factual adherence required for advisory services. To address this, we adopt task-specific optimization strategies tailored to the unique modalities of medical interaction. In this section, we present our specialized methodologies for two core capabilities: Deep Clinical Consultation, where we employ a segmented pipeline and the Step-Penalized Advantage with Relative baseline algorithm to master the multi-turn diagnostic trajectory; and Credible Healthcare Advisory, which utilizes dynamic rubric evolution and Fact-Aware Reinforcement Learning to ensure the safety and verifiability of medical information.

临床实践涵盖一系列多样的认知活动, 从主动采集症状病史到严谨地提供循证建议. 单一整体式的训练方法往往难以兼顾这些场景的不同要求, 尤其是诊断所需的演绎逻辑与咨询服务所需的严格事实遵循. 为此, 我们针对医学交互的不同形态采用任务专项的优化策略. 本节介绍两项核心能力的专门方法: 一是深度临床问诊 (Deep Clinical Consultation), 用分段流水线与 Step-Penalized Advantage with Relative baseline 算法掌握多轮诊断轨迹; 二是可信健康咨询 (Credible Healthcare Advisory), 用动态 rubric 演化与事实感知强化学习 (Fact-Aware Reinforcement Learning) 保证医学信息的安全与可核验.

#### 3.1.2 Policy Learning Algorithm: SPAR 策略学习算法: SPAR

Conventional RL approaches, such as GRPO [20, 40] with global rewards, exhibit significant limitations in long-horizon medical interviewing. These include reward hacking (inflating recall via redundant questions), logic fragmentation (disjointed clinical transitions), and ineffective credit assignment. In long-context dialogues, trajectory-level rewards fail to isolate local errors [41], often inducing training instability by penalizing valid reasoning chains alongside specific flaws.

常规 RL 方法, 例如使用全局奖励的 GRPO [20, 40], 在长程医学问诊中有明显局限. 这些局限包括 reward hacking (靠冗余提问抬高召回), 逻辑碎片化 (临床转接不连贯) 以及低效的信用分配. 在长上下文对话中, 轨迹级奖励无法隔离局部错误 [41], 常常连同具体缺陷一起惩罚有效的推理链, 引发训练不稳定.

To address these challenges, we propose SPAR (Step-Penalized Advantage with Relative baseline), which introduces fine-grained step-wise penalties and a decoupled advantage estimation mechanism to induce an adaptive curriculum-learning effect.

为应对这些挑战, 我们提出 SPAR (Step-Penalized Advantage with Relative baseline). 它引入细粒度的步级惩罚与解耦的优势估计机制, 从而诱导出自适应的课程学习效果.

**Reward Formulation.** SPAR employs a hierarchical reward structure. For a generated response y consisting of L logical interaction steps $[ z _ { 1 } , \ldots , z _ { L } ]$ , we define each step $z _ { j }$ as one complete dialogue exchange (i.e., a user turn followed by the assistant turn). We first compute a Global Reward $R _ { \mathrm { g l o b a l } }$ by evaluating the complete trajectory against a set of pre-defined Clinical Rubrics (e.g., diagnostic accuracy, evidence completeness).

**奖励构成.** SPAR 采用分层的奖励结构. 对一个由 L 个逻辑交互步骤 $[z_1, \ldots, z_L]$ 组成的生成回答 y, 我们把每一步 $z_j$ 定义为一次完整的对话交换 (即一轮用户发言接一轮助手发言). 我们先依据一组预先定义的临床 rubric (例如诊断准确性, 证据完整性) 评估整条轨迹, 计算全局奖励 $R_{\mathrm{global}}$.

Simultaneously, a step verifier performs real-time validation for each interaction step $z _ { j }$ . Let $\mathbb { V } _ { j }$ denote the set of violation types triggered by step $z _ { j }$ (e.g., redundancy, safety risk). We define the step-wise validity factor $\gamma _ { j } \in ( 0 , 1 ]$ as:

与此同时, 一个步骤验证器对每个交互步骤 $z_j$ 做实时校验. 令 $\mathbb{V}_j$ 表示步骤 $z_j$ 触发的违规类型集合 (例如冗余, 安全风险). 我们把步级有效因子 $\gamma_j \in (0, 1]$ 定义为:

$$
\gamma_ {j} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1, & \text {if} \mathbb {V} _ {j} = \emptyset \\ \min _ {v \in \mathbb {V} _ {j}} (\lambda_ {v}), & \text {otherwise} \end{array} \right.\tag{7}
$$

where $\lambda _ { v } \; \in \; ( 0 , 1 )$ is the penalty coefficient for violation type v. This formulation enforces a minimum validity principle: if a step commits multiple errors, only the most severe penalty (smallest λ) is applied. The effective return for the step is then modulated as $R _ { j } = \gamma _ { j } \cdot R _ { \mathrm { g l o b a l } }$

其中 $\lambda_v \in (0, 1)$ 是违规类型 v 的惩罚系数. 这一形式贯彻 「最小有效」 原则: 一个步骤若同时犯下多种错误, 只施加最严重的那一项惩罚 (最小的 λ). 该步骤的有效回报随之调制为 $R_j = \gamma_j \cdot R_{\mathrm{global}}$.

**Step-wise Advantage Estimation.** The core innovation of SPAR lies in its advantage computation, which decouples local penalties from the group baseline. Unlike traditional approaches that normalize rewards using the penalized distribution, SPAR computes the advantage $\hat { A } _ { j }$ for step $z _ { j }$ by comparing the step-penalized return against an unpenalized group average:

**步级优势估计.** SPAR 的核心创新在于优势的计算方式: 它把局部惩罚与组基线解耦. 传统方法用施加惩罚后的分布来归一化奖励, SPAR 则把施加步级惩罚后的回报与未加惩罚的组均值相比, 计算步骤 $z_j$ 的优势 $\hat{A}_j$:

$$

$$

Specifically, we categorize constraints into two distinct classes:

**Admission and Exit Rules** To prevent rule explosion and ensure the reward signal remains potent, the dynamic rubric set follows a “Problem-Driven” lifecycle:

**准入与退出规则** 为防止规则膨胀并保持奖励信号的力度, 动态 rubric 集遵循 「问题驱动」 的生命周期:

#### 3.2.2 Fact-Aware Reinforcement Learning 事实感知强化学习

In the paradigm of Reinforcement Learning with Verification Rewards (RLVR), naive hallucination suppression strategies typically convert verification signals directly into scalar penalties [29, 46]. The standard objective form is defined as

在带验证奖励的强化学习 (RLVR) 范式中, 朴素的幻觉抑制策略通常把验证信号直接转成标量惩罚 [29, 46]. 标准的目标形式定义为

$$
R = R _ {t a s k} + \alpha \cdot R _ {h a l l u}\tag{9}
$$

Here, $R _ { h a l l u }$ utilizes a count-based hallucination rate metric $( - N _ { \mathrm { h a l l u } } / N _ { \mathrm { t o t a l } } )$ . While this objective aims to preserve core medical reasoning capability $( R _ { t a s k } )$ while reducing hallucinations via a penalty term, it is vulnerable to two major forms of reward hacking in long-form generation:

这里, $R_{hallu}$ 采用基于计数的幻觉率指标 ($-N_{\mathrm{hallu}}/N_{\mathrm{total}}$). 这个目标希望在保留核心医学推理能力 ($R_{task}$) 的同时, 通过惩罚项减少幻觉; 但在长文本生成中, 它容易遭到两种主要的 reward hacking:

• **Redundancy-Induced Dilution:** The model inflates the denominator $N _ { \mathrm { t o t a l } }$ by producing many factually correct but low-value statements, thereby reducing the measured hallucination rate without correcting the core errors.

• **冗余稀释:** 模型输出大量事实正确但价值不高的陈述, 抬高分母 $N_{\mathrm{total}}$, 在不纠正核心错误的情况下拉低测得的幻觉率.

• **Penalty-Induced Conservatism:** Strict penalties can encourage overly conservative strategies (e.g., shortening outputs to avoid penalties), which undermines exploration and the acquisition of complex reasoning behaviors.

• **惩罚诱发的保守:** 严格的惩罚会鼓励过度保守的策略 (例如缩短输出以规避惩罚), 损害探索, 也妨碍模型习得复杂的推理行为.

To address these issues, we propose a joint optimization framework comprising Structured Signal Denoising and Dynamic Multi-Objective Aggregation, as shown in Fig. 3.

为解决这些问题, 我们提出一个联合优化框架, 由结构化信号去噪 (Structured Signal Denoising) 与动态多目标聚合 (Dynamic Multi-Objective Aggregation) 组成, 如 Fig. 3 所示.

**Structured Signal Denoising** To establish a reward signal robust to redundancy, we reformulate the verification of atomic claims as a weighted evaluation based on semantic density. This ensures that hallucinations in core diagnostic statements incur significantly higher penalties than those in marginal content.

**结构化信号去噪** 为建立对冗余稳健的奖励信号, 我们把原子论断的验证重新表述为一种基于语义密度的加权评估. 这保证核心诊断陈述里的幻觉比边缘内容里的幻觉受到重得多的惩罚.

We first map the response sequences $\{ s _ { j } \}$ and extracted claims $\{ c _ { i } \}$ into a vector space via a semantic encoder $\mathcal { E } ( \cdot )$ . To prevent the manipulation of metrics through synonymous paraphrasing, we apply semantic clustering with a cosine similarity threshold. By selecting a representative claim $c _ { k } ^ { * }$ for each cluster $\mathcal { C } _ { k } ,$ we transition the evaluation metric from lexical frequency to semantic unit density. We then quantify the information contribution of each claim using a Saliency Weight, $w ( c _ { k } ^ { * } )$ , defined as its maximum semantic correlation across the response sentences:

我们先通过语义编码器 $\mathcal{E}(\cdot)$ 把回答的句子序列 $\{s_j\}$ 与抽取出的论断 $\{c_i\}$ 映射到向量空间. 为防止通过同义改写操纵指标, 我们以余弦相似度阈值做语义聚类. 为每个簇 $\mathcal{C}_k$ 选出一条代表论断 $c_k^*$, 评估指标就从词面频次转到语义单元密度. 随后, 我们用显著性权重 $w(c_k^*)$ 量化每条论断的信息贡献, 定义为它与回答各句之间的最大语义相关度:

$$

The factuality reward $R _ { f a c t }$ is computed as a weighted penalty term:

事实性奖励 $R_{fact}$ 按加权惩罚项计算:

$$
R _ {f a c t} = - \frac {\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {K} w (c _ {k} ^ {*}) \cdot \mathbb {I} (c _ {k} ^ {*})}{\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {K} w (c _ {k} ^ {*}) + \epsilon}\tag{11}
$$

where $\mathbb { I } ( c _ { k } ^ { * } )$ is the verification penalty indicator defined as:

其中 $\mathbb{I}(c_k^*)$ 为验证惩罚指示量, 定义为:

$$
\mathbb {I} (c _ {k} ^ {*}) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1 & \text {if} c _ {k} ^ {*} \in \{\text {Refuted, Uncertain} \} \\ 0 & \text {otherwise} \end{array} \right.\tag{12}
$$

Mechanically, the weighted denominator neutralizes the inflation of claim counts (anti-dilution), while the saliency-dependent numerator ensures that penalties are concentrated on core errors rather than marginal text, thereby preserving reasoning utility.

从机制上看, 加权分母抵消了论断条数的膨胀 (防稀释), 而依赖显著性的分子确保惩罚集中在核心错误而不是边缘文字上, 从而保住推理的实用性.

> **拆开:** 式 (10) 的显著性权重取论断与回答各句余弦相似度的最大值. 论断本来就是从这些句子里抽出来的, 每条都能找到一句与它高度相似的原句, 权重靠什么区分核心与边缘?
> 拆开看, 式 (10)-(11) 做了两件事. 第一件是语义聚类, 把同义复述并成一个代表论断 $c_k^*$, 分母按簇计而不按条数计, 这一步直接压住了 「换说法凑条数」; 第二件才是按 $w(c_k^*)$ 加权. 由于论断与其来源句同源, $w$ 大多会落在接近 1 的区间, 只有当来源句很长, 论断只占其中一小部分, 或者论断经过指代消解改写较多时, 余弦值才会明显下降. 所以 「核心错误受罚更重」 主要取决于编码器 $\mathcal{E}$ 与句子切分的粒度, 正文没有给出 $w$ 的分布. 另外, 模型若堆砌大量正确但低价值的论断, 只要它们各自成簇且 $w$ 不低, 分母照样会变大; 「anti-dilution」 的效果更多来自聚类, 而不是加权.

We introduce a softgating coefficient, $\lambda ( R _ { t a s k } )$ , which modulates the penalty intensity based on the task reward achieved by the on-policy generated response.

**动态多目标聚合** 为在强化医学推理与抑制幻觉之间取得平衡, 我们实现一个动态聚合机制. 我们引入软门控系数 $\lambda(R_{task})$, 它依据 on-policy 生成的回答所获得的任务奖励, 调节惩罚强度.

#### 4.2.2 HealthBench-Hallu

While benchmarks such as HealthBench have established standards for evaluating medical LLMs, ensuring clinical safety remains paramount for healthcare AI deployment. Medical hallucina tions, which involve the generation of superficially plausible yet factually incorrect information, represent a significant concern acknowledged by regulatory authorities and the research community [17, 50, 51]. This issue is particularly pronounced in complex medical reasoning tasks, where model outputs involve extended passages with high information density, encompassing specialized knowledge such as precise pharmaceutical dosing and rare clinical presentations. The combination of extended generation lengths and linguistic coherence creates conditions wherein fine-grained factual errors may evade detection, thereby presenting substantial clinical risks. We therefore introduce HealthBench-Hallu, a fine-grained evaluation framework designed to assess the factual integrity of model responses generated across HealthBench tasks. By decomposing these outputs into discrete atomic claims and validating them against external evidence, this metric provides a rigorous quantification of medical hallucinations.

尽管 HealthBench 等基准已为评估医学大模型建立了标准, 确保临床安全仍是医疗 AI 部署的首要问题. 医学幻觉是指生成表面合理但事实错误的信息, 监管机构与研究界都承认这是一个重大隐患 [17, 50, 51]. 这一问题在复杂医学推理任务中尤为突出: 模型输出篇幅长, 信息密度高, 涉及精确的给药剂量, 罕见的临床表现等专业知识. 篇幅长加上语言连贯, 细粒度的事实错误就可能逃过检查, 带来实质性的临床风险. 因此我们提出 HealthBench-Hallu, 一个细粒度评估框架, 用来评估模型在 HealthBench 任务上所生成回答的事实完整性. 通过把输出拆成离散的原子论断并依据外部证据核验, 这一指标对医学幻觉做出严格的量化.

• **Fabricated or distorted medical evidence:** Inventing non-existent references, falsifying clin-

**HealthBench-Hallu Evaluation** HealthBench-Hallu reuses the Fact-Aware Verification Pipeline (see Section 2.2.2), but runs it in a high-precision (rather than high-throughput) configuration: we upgrade the claim extractor to GPT-5 to improve coverage of fine-grained atomic claims, and enforce real-time multi-turn search verification instead of relying on cached evidence. Based on above evidence labels generated by this verification pipeline, we calculate the Weighted Hallucination Rate (H):

**HealthBench-Hallu 评测** HealthBench-Hallu 复用事实感知验证流水线 (见第 2.2.2 节), 但以高精度 (而非高吞吐) 配置运行: 我们把论断抽取器升级为 GPT-5, 提高对细粒度原子论断的覆盖, 并强制使用实时多轮检索验证, 而不依赖缓存的证据. 依据该验证流水线生成的证据标签, 我们计算加权幻觉率 (H):

$$
H = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {N} w _ {i}}{| \text {Total Claims} |}\tag{15}
$$

The weights $w _ { i }$ are assigned based on factual risk stratification: **Refuted** (1.0) represents severe factual fallacies; **Uncertain** (0.5) represents statements with insufficient evidence or ambiguity risks; and **Supported** (0.0) denotes correct statements. This metric directly reflects the credibilit boundaries of the model when addressing complex medical problems.

权重 $w_i$ 按事实风险分层赋值: **Refuted** (1.0) 代表严重的事实谬误; **Uncertain** (0.5) 代表证据不足或存在歧义风险的陈述; **Supported** (0.0) 表示正确的陈述. 这一指标直接反映模型处理复杂医学问题时的可信边界.

## 5 Inference Optimization 推理优化

To facilitate the accessibility and efficient deployment of the Baichuan-M3 model in healthcare applications, two inference optimized strategies are implemented. First, to accelerate text generation, the Gated Eagle-3 speculative decoding approach was introduced, leading to a substantial improvement in inference throughput. Second, advanced quantization methods were applied to significantly reduce the memory requirements of the model without compromising accuracy. These inference optimizations lower the practical barriers to deployment and support broader applications of advanced medical large language models.

为便于 Baichuan-M3 在医疗应用中的可及与高效部署, 我们实施了两项推理优化策略. 第一, 为加速文本生成, 我们引入 Gated Eagle-3 投机解码方法, 显著提升推理吞吐. 第二, 我们应用先进的量化方法, 在不损失精度的前提下大幅降低模型的显存需求. 这些推理优化降低了实际部署的门槛, 支持先进医学大模型的更广泛应用.

### 5.1 Speculative Decoding 投机解码

Speculative decoding is an inference-time acceleration technique for autoregressive generation. It uses a lightweight draft model to propose multiple candidate tokens, which are then verified in parallel by the target model. The algorithm accepts the longest verified prefix and discards the remaining candidates, allowing the target model to commit multiple tokens per verification step and thereby increasing decoding throughput.

投机解码是一种面向自回归生成的推理阶段加速技术. 它用一个轻量的草稿模型提出多个候选 token, 再由目标模型并行验证. 算法接受验证通过的最长前缀, 丢弃其余候选, 使目标模型在每个验证步中确认多个 token, 从而提高解码吞吐.

In the Eagle-3 [52] framework, the draft model is additionally conditioned on hidden states from the target model to leverage richer semantic information. However, the pronounced capacity gap between the target and draft models can induce a representation mismatch: high-dimensional and information-dense hidden states may overwhelm the lightweight draft model, reducing its ability to effectively exploit the auxiliary signal. This often leads to lower candidate acceptance rates and consequently limits the achievable speedup.

在 Eagle-3 [52] 框架中, 草稿模型还额外以目标模型的隐藏状态为条件, 以利用更丰富的语义信息. 然而, 目标模型与草稿模型之间明显的容量差距会造成表示失配: 高维且信息密集的隐藏状态可能让轻量的草稿模型不堪重负, 削弱它有效利用这一辅助信号的能力. 这常常导致候选接受率降低, 进而限制可达到的加速比.

To mitigate this issue, we incorporate a Gated-Attention [53] module into the Eagle-3 draft model (called Gated Eagle-3; see Fig. 10), providing a dynamic and learnable mechanism to regulate the injected information. Concretely, the attention output is routed through a gating unit and modulated via element-wise multiplication with a gate vector that is dynamically generated from the current-layer input. This design enables fine-grained, dimension-wise control of information flow, emphasizing salient features while suppressing redundant or noisy components.

为缓解这一问题, 我们在 Eagle-3 草稿模型中加入门控注意力 (Gated-Attention) [53] 模块 (称为 Gated Eagle-3; 见 Fig. 10), 为注入的信息提供一种动态, 可学习的调节机制. 具体来说, 注意力输出经过一个门控单元, 与一个由当前层输入动态生成的门向量逐元素相乘. 这一设计实现了细粒度的, 逐维度的信息流控制, 强化显著特征, 抑制冗余或噪声成分.

As detailed in Appendix A.4, Gated Eagle-3 achieves an average acceptance length improvement of 0.31 and improves average throughput by 12% over the Eagle-3 base. These results indicate that selectively regulating target-model information translates more reliably into practical decoding acceleration, providing useful insights for future improvements.

如附录 A.4 所述, Gated Eagle-3 相比 Eagle-3 基线, 平均接受长度提升 0.31, 平均吞吐提升 12%. 这些结果表明, 有选择地调节来自目标模型的信息, 能更可靠地转化为实际的解码加速, 为后续改进提供了有用的启示.

### 5.2 Quantization 量化

To reduce GPU memory consumption for Baichuan-M3 in resource-constrained settings, we apply INT4 weight quantization. However, the sparsely activated nature of Mixture-of-Experts models poses challenges for standard quantization calibration [54]: calibration corpora typically activate only a subset of experts, resulting in biased calibration. Consequently, frequently activated experts are quantized accurately, whereas rarely activated experts incur larger quantization errors, which can lead to unstable and unpredictable accuracy degradation at inference time.

为降低 Baichuan-M3 在资源受限场景下的显存占用, 我们采用 INT4 权重量化. 然而, MoE 模型稀疏激活的特性给标准的量化校准带来挑战 [54]: 校准语料通常只激活一部分专家, 导致校准有偏. 结果是, 频繁激活的专家量化得很准, 很少激活的专家量化误差较大, 可能在推理时造成不稳定且难以预料的精度下降.

To address this issue, we propose a self-generated calibration scheme that promotes uniform expert coverage. Specifically, we construct a multi-domain prompt set and use the BF16 model to generate high-quality responses for calibration. This approach offers two key benefits: (i) the diverse prompts activate nearly all experts, providing sufficient samples for each expert and mitigating calibration bias; and (ii) the self-generated responses encourage the quantized model to match the output distribution of the BF16 model, thereby reducing distributional discrepancies. The training of quantization parameters was based on AutoRound [55] framework, and the quantization format adheres to the GPTQ [56] standard.

为解决这一问题, 我们提出一种自生成的校准方案, 让专家覆盖更均匀. 具体来说, 我们构建一个多领域的 prompt 集合, 用 BF16 模型生成高质量回答作为校准数据. 这种做法有两个主要好处: (i) 多样的 prompt 几乎能激活所有专家, 为每个专家提供充足样本, 缓解校准偏差; (ii) 自生成的回答促使量化模型贴合 BF16 模型的输出分布, 减少分布差异. 量化参数的训练基于 AutoRound [55] 框架, 量化格式遵循 GPTQ [56] 标准.

## 6 Conclusion

We introduce **Baichuan-M3**, a medical-enhanced large language model that unifies clinical inquiry with reliable medical decision-making. By explicitly modeling the clinical workflow, Baichuan-M3 enables proactive information acquisition, coherent multi-step reasoning, and effective hallucination suppression. Through a three-stage training paradigm combining task-specific reinforcement learning and multi-teacher distillation, the model achieves strong performance on both factual and process-oriented benchmarks, including HealthBench, HealthBench-Hallu and the OSCE-style ScanBench. These results demonstrate that workflow-aligned optimization is an effective approach for advancing clinical-grade medical LLMs.

我们提出 **Baichuan-M3**, 一个经过医学增强的大语言模型, 把临床问诊与可靠的医学决策统一起来. 通过显式建模临床流程, Baichuan-M3 实现了主动的信息获取, 连贯的多步推理与有效的幻觉抑制. 借助把任务专项强化学习与多教师蒸馏结合起来的三阶段训练范式, 模型在事实类与过程类基准上都取得强劲表现, 包括 HealthBench, HealthBench-Hallu 与 OSCE 式的 ScanBench. 这些结果表明, 与临床流程对齐的优化是推进临床级医学大模型的有效途径.

## 7 Limitation and Future Work 局限与未来工作

Baichuan-M3 is currently limited to episodic, text-based clinical scenarios and does not fully cap ture longitudinal disease management, multimodal clinical signals, or ultra-long-horizon reasoning across patient trajectories. While hallucination control is substantially improved, rare high-risk errors and limited explicit grounding in evidence-based sources remain open challenges. Future work will focus on extending the model toward full-pathway clinical reasoning with multimodal inputs, long-context optimization, and tighter integration of evidence retrieval, safety constraints, and environment-based reinforcement learning.

Baichuan-M3 目前局限于单次就诊式的, 基于文本的临床场景, 尚不能完整覆盖纵向的疾病管理, 多模态临床信号, 以及贯穿患者全病程的超长程推理. 幻觉控制虽已大幅改善, 罕见的高风险错误, 以及对循证来源的显式依据不足, 仍是有待解决的难题. 未来工作将着力把模型扩展到结合多模态输入的全路径临床推理, 优化长上下文, 并把证据检索, 安全约束与基于环境的强化学习更紧密地整合起来.

### A.2 Fact-Aware RL 事实感知 RL

#### A.2.1 Evaluation of Distilled Claim Extraction Models 蒸馏论断抽取模型的评估

While offline claim extraction relies on GPT-5 as the reference extractor, deploying such a large teacher model for online reinforcement learning is computationally prohibitive.
